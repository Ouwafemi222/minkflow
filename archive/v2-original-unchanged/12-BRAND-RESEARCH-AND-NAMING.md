# Brand naming and domain due diligence — OPEN DECISION

## Founder constraints
Short, uncommon, simple, pronounceable, memorable, internationally usable, easy to type after hearing once; avoid generic AI buzzwords and platform-specific brands. Founder suggestions: **Animink**, **YouTube Mink**; neither is approved. Do not choose a name on behalf of founder.

## Research already established
- Animink is an existing animation-origin digital design/software company founded in 1998 with active `animink.com`: https://www.animink.com/company/ . Treat as a material conflict, not available.
- YouTube Mink: no verified clearance; avoid embedding YouTube (third-party trademark) in core brand without legal review.
- Creative candidates requiring checks: Minkora, Animivo, Viminko, Minkflow, Anivora, Minkframe, Anivelo. These are brainstorming ONLY; none is confirmed available.

## Required pre-approval research for EACH finalist
1. Exact and phonetic search for business names, products, mobile apps and animation/video SaaS competitors; record URLs and date.
2. Registry/RDAP and registrar checkout check for .com, .app, .ai, .io; mark AVAILABLE, TAKEN, PREMIUM, or UNVERIFIED with evidence. A search engine's lack of results is NOT availability.
3. Trademark searches in relevant markets (Nigeria, US, UK, WIPO) including confusingly similar marks and related classes; consult qualified counsel when material.
4. Check GitHub/npm names and YouTube, X, Instagram, TikTok handles; no assumptions based on inaccessible pages.
5. Say/pronounce/spell tests with target users; rank distinctiveness, recall, spelling, domain, trademark risk and growth flexibility.
6. Present top 3 with evidence and tradeoffs; request explicit founder selection BEFORE branding code, domains, logos, or launch collateral.
