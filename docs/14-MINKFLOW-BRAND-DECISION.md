# Minkflow — Founder-approved product name (V3)

**Decision:** Founder selected **Minkflow** for the AI animated explainer video SaaS on 2026-10-30. Use this name in product copy, dashboard, navigation, emails, marketing drafts, SEO titles, and watermark labels. **This is naming approval, not proof of domain or trademark availability.**

## Brand system
- Brand display name: `Minkflow` (capital M, lowercase rest).
- Internal codename: `minkflow` (only for new code identifiers where appropriate).
- Positioning: AI-powered animated explainer studio for creators, educators and businesses.
- Product promise: Turn a topic or script into an editable, narrated 2D explainer video.
- Draft tagline: `Ideas into motion.` (provisional; not a legally cleared slogan).
- Suggested config: `PRODUCT_NAME=Minkflow`, `NEXT_PUBLIC_PRODUCT_NAME=Minkflow`; centralize brand in one `brand.ts` or config module.
- Domain, URLs, callback hosts, email domains, OAuth, and payments merchant identity: use environment-based placeholders until verified and owned. **Do not assume `minkflow.com` is available.**
- Do not replace legacy brand strings inside historical archived documents; historical references are preserved for traceability. In active source files, use Minkflow.

## Naming verification before external launch
1. Independently verify live registrar/RDAP domain status for desired TLDs and checkout price.
2. Search existing brands, relevant trademarks (Nigeria, WIPO, US/UK as applicable), phonetic similarity and competing SaaS names.
3. Check handles and product naming collisions; record evidence/date.
4. Obtain founder approval before buying a domain, trademark filing or public launch.
5. If conflicts emerge, flag them immediately; do not silently rename the product.
