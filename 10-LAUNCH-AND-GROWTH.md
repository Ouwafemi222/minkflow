# Launch and customer validation

## Before writing significant code
Interview 20 YouTube automation creators (Nigeria plus international comparison). Ask for recent projects, current tools, cost per minute, turnaround, hardest editing step, desired style and willingness to pay. Do not lead with feature ideas. Collect permissioned sample scripts and target output examples.

## Beta recruitment
Landing page + 30–60 second demo video; waitlist; invite 10–20 creators; concierge onboarding. Provide bounded trial credits. Track actual usage and interview every tester after first export.

## Launch positioning
'From script to editable animated explainer — without a complicated timeline.' Avoid 'guaranteed viral' or 'guaranteed monetization' claims. Emphasize originality and creator control.

## Acquisition experiments
- Direct outreach to YouTube automation communities and educators.
- Short demos: prompt -> storyboard -> editable scene -> final output.
- Tutorials comparing production time (with transparent methodology).
- Referral credit experiment after abuse protections.
- Templates for finance, tech, history and business education.

## Metrics and decisions
Activation = first successful export. North star = successful exported minutes with user-rated acceptable quality. Track first-video time, export failure rate, cost/minute, scene regeneration, weekly active creators, paid conversion, retention and refunds.

## Decision gates
- If fewer than 5/20 interviewed creators express meaningful interest, narrow persona/problem before more coding.
- If template quality scores low, invest in visual system rather than adding features.
- If per-minute cost exceeds willingness to pay, reduce generated imagery or change pricing.
- If users demand cinematic output, validate as separate premium tier, not silent MVP expansion.

## Brand diligence
Search exact/phonetic names in target countries, relevant trademark classes, domain registries, app stores, social handles and SEO results. Do not assume Scenova is available; keep product copy name-configurable.
