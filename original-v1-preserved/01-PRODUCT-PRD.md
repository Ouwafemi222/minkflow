# Product requirements document

## Vision and positioning
Scenova helps creators produce original, coherent, animated educational explainers faster than manual production. AI is an assistant; users retain control of scripts, scene layouts, voice and final export. The competitive advantage is consistent storytelling, editability, and transparent per-minute costs—not unlimited one-click video generation.

## Personas
- Solo faceless YouTube creator: low budget, repeats weekly, needs branded videos.
- Educational content creator: accuracy, readable visuals, narration and captions.
- Small agency: reusable styles, reliable delivery and export quality.

## Jobs to be done
1. Convert topic to structured script (hook, body, examples, outro) or import existing script.
2. Approve script and create timed storyboard with scene purpose, visual treatment and narration.
3. Generate/select visual assets, TTS and word timings.
4. Edit scene text, layout, assets, narration, timing, transitions and style.
5. Preview quickly and render final MP4 with reliable progress and retry.

## MVP must-haves
- Email login, account, workspace (single owner), project list and project autosave.
- New project wizard: topic OR pasted script, audience, tone, duration, language=en, 16:9.
- Script generation and user approval; explicit 'generate storyboard' action.
- Typed scene schema, scene reorder, scene-level regenerate, 8–12 animation templates.
- Voice selection from licensed TTS providers, narration preview, word/segment alignment.
- Per-scene preview, low-res draft preview, 1080p final render and secure download.
- Credits estimate before generation, reservation, final settlement and usage ledger.
- Jobs dashboard with queued/running/completed/failed/canceled, actionable error messaging.
- Brand kit: colors, fonts, logo (optional); captions on/off.
- Admin diagnostics and abuse controls.

## Not in MVP
- 3D animation, cinematic AI video clips, auto-upload, marketplace, voice cloning, real-time team collaboration, mobile-native app, non-English voice support, stock licensing brokerage.

## Definition of done
- User creates a coherent 60-second video from topic and exports it without engineer help.
- 1–5-minute videos render with stable timing, no blank frames, correct aspect ratio and captions.
- Scene edits do not regenerate unaffected scenes.
- Every paid job has a traceable credit reservation, usage events, final settlement and refund on failed work.
- Users cannot access another user's project, storage object, render or usage history.
- A failed worker job is retryable without duplicate charges or corrupted artifacts.

## Content and ethics
Creators must review claims and copyrighted materials. Provide original-content guidance, licensed asset attribution and AI voice disclosure guidance. Prohibit deceptive impersonation, unlawful content and unauthorized copyrighted assets. No claims of guaranteed YouTube monetization.
